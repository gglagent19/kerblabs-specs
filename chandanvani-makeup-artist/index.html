The idea

A first impression that fits the artist.

Chandanvani Makeup Artist is a bridal makeup artist in Jalandhar with a 51K Instagram following, and the bio says she is available globally. Today there is no website, so the Instagram profile and a phone number do all the work. This concept shows how the same brand could greet a bride on the web: polished, confident, and one tap from a WhatsApp message.
