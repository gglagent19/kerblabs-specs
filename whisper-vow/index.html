The Studio

An Austin practice, quietly built.

Whisper & Vow is a small, by-appointment studio for bridal boudoir, intimate weddings, and elopements. Founded and led by Candace — with a focus on the slow, intentional kind of imagery that holds up at the end of a long day, and a long life.

Services

Three things, done with care.

Different occasions, the same considered hand — styling, posing, light, and time.

01

Bridal Boudoir

A pre-wedding portrait session in the studio — with styling guidance, professional hair & makeup, gentle posing direction, and a same-day reveal. The album becomes a private gift, or simply a held keepsake.

02

Intimate Weddings

For small ceremonies of twenty or fewer. Coverage of the morning prep, the vows, the dinner, and the quiet in-between moments that get missed at larger weddings.

03

Elopements

For couples who choose just-the-two-of-us, or just-the-two-of-us-and-witnesses. Austin courthouse, Hill Country backdrop, or somewhere only you two know. Full-day coverage included.

The Experience

One afternoon, start to print.

A full experience — consultation, styling, the session, and a same-day reveal.

Consult
