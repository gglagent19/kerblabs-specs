The Studio

A Brooklyn practice, solo-led.

Evgenia Ribinik Studios is a 5-star Brooklyn boudoir, bridal, and portrait photography studio inside Industry City. Solo-led, women-owned, LGBTQ+ friendly. Sessions are booked, not browsed; the work is editorial; the studio is the kind of room reviewers describe as comfortable in 28 separate handwritings.
