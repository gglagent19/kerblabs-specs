What does MTB Exotics rent?

Exotic and luxury cars listed on its site include the Lamborghini Urus, Huracan Evo and Huracan Spyder, Ferrari 488 GTB Spyder, Rolls Royce Dawn and Ghost, Corvette C8 Z06 and Mercedes-Benz G63 AMG.

What are the listed 24-hour specials?

Per the site: Huracan Evo $1,599, Huracan Spyder $1,699, Ferrari 488 GTB Spyder $1,799, Rolls Royce Dawn $1,799, Rolls Royce Ghost $1,499, Corvette C8 Z06 $599.

Do they deliver?

The site lists delivery within the DFW area at $200 round trip.

What are the rental requirements?

Per the site: 21 or older (surcharge under 25), a United States drivers license, full coverage insurance, and a refundable security deposit of $1,000 to $2,000. 100 miles per day are included; extra miles are $3 to $5.
