The Studio

Editorial portraiture, quietly made.

Purple Boudoir is a fine-art portrait studio in Chicago’s West Loop. Light, silk, walnut, and the slow patience of a single afternoon. Every session is a one-on-one with Jacob — founder, lead photographer — and his team. Tasteful, discreet, considered: a portrait you would hang as art.
