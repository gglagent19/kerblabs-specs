The Studio

A Las Vegas studio, by appointment.

Boudoir Cafe is a 5-star Las Vegas boudoir photography studio — women-owned, LGBTQ+ friendly, and led by Cherie & Hedley. Sessions are booked, not browsed; the work is editorial; the room is built for comfort first and camera second.

The Duo

Two photographers, one room.

A reviewer recently put it simply: “Cherie and Hedley are truly exceptional at what they do. From the moment we met, I felt comfortable and at ease.” The studio runs as a duo — with the kind of attentiveness that 15 separate reviews describe with the same phrase: comfortable atmosphere.

Cherie

Lead Photographer · Founder

Cherie leads the session — styling direction, light, posing, the steady hand that turns a first-timer’s nerves into a portrait the client wants to print. Listed online and in reviews as “Cheriefoto.”

Hedley

Studio Partner

Hedley handles the room — the welcome, the wardrobe staging, the steady presence that makes a boudoir session feel less like a shoot and more like an afternoon with a friend who happens to be exceptional at this.
