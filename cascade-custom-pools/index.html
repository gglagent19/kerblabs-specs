What they build

Listed on their own site. The headline ones:

Custom gunite pools

Design and construction, from modern to freeform and resort-style layouts, with 3D pool design, excavation and engineering.

Tile, coping, decking

Finish work around the shell, plus spas and water features.

Outdoor living

Patios, stone decks, pergolas, cabanas, outdoor kitchens, fire features and integrated lighting.

Four decades, one family

Cascade Custom Pools lists more than 40 years in business, founded 1982 by Hank Thompson, with Theresa Thompson and T.J. Thompson (joined 2008). It serves Austin, Dripping Springs, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Spicewood, West Lake Hills, Fredericksburg, Rollingwood, Volente, Steiner Ranch and surrounding Hill Country communities, per cascadecustompools.com.
