The room

One room. No schedule conflicts.

That is how Boulevard describes itself: your session from start to finish. Studio hours are Monday to Friday, 10am to 10pm, weekends by arrangement, and after hours they say just ask.

Console

Sound Techniques ZR36, 36 input channels with passive inductor EQ, installed in 2023. The studio notes fewer than 20 are known to exist.

Mic & keys

A Telefunken U48 microphone and a 1917 Bechstein grand piano.

Drums & amps

A 1959 Ludwig drum kit, vintage amps and an outboard rack.
