Tours from Marathon to Key West

Up The Keys Tours is a family-run outfit at 6810 Front St, Key West. Their site lists day trips up the Keys, Key West walking tours and an evening tiki bar crawl, with free pick-up and drop-off at Key West stays and vacation rentals. Booking runs through FareHarbor, or by phone.

Day tours

The 4 hour Nature Tour from Key West, adults $119, children $35. Aquarium Encounters and Dolphin Research Center day tours, adults $159, children $35. Marathon to Key West private day tour, $549 for up to 6.

On foot

Welcome to Key West and Haunted History walking tours, $275 for up to 6 people, $35 for each additional guest. Groups of 12 or more call 305-928-8961.

After dark

The Florida Keys Tiki Bar Crawl visits three tiki bars, $350 for up to 4 people, adults only.
