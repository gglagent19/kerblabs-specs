Flagship Lodge

All-inclusive, on the banks of East Rosebud Creek. The lodge site says it has operated since 1947.

Spring Creek Lodge

A remote property in a secluded mountain valley with private spring creek access. Listed as operating since 1937.

Bighorn River Lodge

A tailwater fishery on the Bighorn River near Fort Smith, Montana. Listed as opened in 2022.

Getting there

Closer than it looks

The lodge sits in the Yellowstone River Basin of south-central Montana, about 60 miles southwest of Billings and 75 minutes from Billings Logan International (BIL), with complimentary transfers listed. Bozeman Yellowstone International (BZN) is about 2.5 hours away.
