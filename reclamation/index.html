The Studio

A Santa Monica room, solo-led.

Reclamation Boudoir is a 4.9-star Santa Monica studio on Main Street — a small, considered space for the kind of portrait that doesn’t announce itself but holds the room when you walk past it on the wall. Sessions are by appointment. Online booking is available.

A portrait is not a record of how you looked. It is a record of how you felt seen.

The Experience

From the consult to the gallery.

A complete studio experience — consultation, styling, the session, and the reveal. Online booking available.

I

Consult

A pre-shoot conversation to talk through your vision, comfort level, and the look you want to leave with. Styling guidance follows.

II

Studio Day

Hair and makeup arranged in advance. By the time the camera comes out, the room has already been set for you.

III

The Session

Relaxed, gently directed, paced like a long exhale. The kind of session that ends and you don’t want to leave.

IV

Reveal

Choose your favorites in the studio. Prints and albums available; gallery delivery is simple and discrete.
