02 / 06 — Our Story

The art and science of beauty.

In 2019, two nurse practitioners and best friends opened the doors of a small boutique in Hyde Park Village — with a simple intention: do fewer treatments, do them better, and grow old with the people who sit in the chair.

Katrina Riley and Lindsey Cronk built ÉLAN around a combined fifteen years of injecting experience, continuous education across the U.S. and Europe, and the belief that great aesthetics is built one careful millimetre at a time.

Katrina serves as a National Educator and Injector Trainer for Galderma Aesthetics — teaching nurse practitioners, physician assistants and physicians the techniques she uses every day in our Tampa studio.
