How we approach roofing websites

We design for one moment: a homeowner standing in their driveway after a storm, phone in hand, deciding who to call. Everything on the page serves that decision, the quote request, the proof of local jobs, the license badges, the one-tap call. We strip out everything that slows that homeowner down.

What a roofing website actually needs

  • An instant quote / inspection request form front and center
  • Before-and-after galleries that prove the workmanship
  • Financing options shown clearly
  • An emergency / storm-damage call-to-action
  • A service-area map so homeowners know you cover them
  • License, insurance and review badges for instant trust

Where most roofing sites lose customers

  • Leads bounce because the quote form is three clicks deep
  • The site doesn't load fast on a phone in a driveway
  • No proof of past jobs, so homeowners default to the big franchise
