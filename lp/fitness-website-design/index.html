How we approach fitness studio websites

Studios sell energy and community, so we make the site feel like the room. Reels, coach profiles and a class schedule carry the vibe, and a free-trial flow turns your Instagram traffic into sign-ups. Built mobile-first because that is where your future members are scrolling.
