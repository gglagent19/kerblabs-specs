How we approach dental practice websites

A practice site has to do two jobs at once: reassure and convert. We design calm, modern, fast pages that signal quality, then make booking effortless with requests that route to your front desk after hours. Service and insurance clarity up front means fewer drop-offs and more booked chairs.

What a dental practice website actually needs

  • Online appointment requests that reach the front desk
  • Clear service pages (implants, aligners, cosmetic)
  • New-patient offers and insurance info up front
  • Smile galleries and patient results
  • Fast, calm, mobile-first design
  • Reviews and credentials that build trust

Where most dental practice sites lose customers

  • The site looks dated and undermines a high-end practice
  • New patients can't book without calling during work hours
  • Service and insurance info is buried, so people bounce
