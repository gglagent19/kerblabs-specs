FAQ

Quick answers

What does First Class Exotics rent?

A fleet its site lists at 48+ vehicles, including Lamborghini, Ferrari, McLaren, Porsche, Rolls-Royce, Mercedes-AMG, Bentley and Corvette models.

What does it cost?

The site lists exotic rentals starting at $799 per day. Rates for specific cars are by request.

Do they deliver?

Yes. White-glove delivery to a hotel, home or venue in Orange County, Los Angeles or San Diego, with same-day delivery available, per the site.

What are the requirements?

Renters must be 25 or older with a valid license and full coverage insurance, plus a major credit card for the security deposit.

How do I book?

Submit the reservation request form on firstclassexotics.com, call (949) 294-5958 or email ali@firstclassexotics.com. No payment is required to inquire.
