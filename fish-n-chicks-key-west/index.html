FAQ

Quick answers

What boat is it?

A 25ft Robalo Cayman with a 300 HP engine. Founder Captain Raquel Crummitt lists more than 25 years on the waters around the Florida Keys, and the site says additional captains are available.

What trips are offered?

Sunset cruises, dolphin watching, sandbar tours, snorkeling and scattering ashes at sea.

How do I book?

Online through FareHarbor on fishnchickskeywest.com, or call (904) 994-5824.

What does it cost?

The current site does not publish prices. Confirm with the captain.
