FAQ

Quick answers

What does Garage Customs FL offer?

Paint protection film, vinyl car wrapping, ceramic coating, window tinting and wheel powder coating in Doral, FL, serving Miami and South Florida.

Which brands are used?

PPF from XPEL, STEK DYNOshield and Stealth; wraps from 3M, Avery Dennison and Inozetek; Ceramic Pro coatings; XPEL window tint.

Can I walk in?

No, there is a no walk-in policy. Monday to Saturday 10 am to 7 pm; Sundays and after hours by appointment.
