Cascio Motors

Their site describes a family-run dealership at 8455 N 90th St in Scottsdale selling pre-owned classic, luxury and exotic vehicles. Joey Cascio has owned and operated it since 2008, with no sales pressure and a warm welcome promised. Visits are by appointment.

Buy or sell

Help buying or selling a vehicle, trade-in valuation, test-drive scheduling and online credit approval, per their site.

Look after it

Restoration, detailing, window tinting, wraps and collection management.

Move it
