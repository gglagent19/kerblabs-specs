The bikes

Chapman Cycles builds custom bicycles by hand in Pawtuxet Village, Rhode Island. Their site shows lugged frames, silver brazing, modified and hand carved fork crowns, and builds for different riding.

City and touring

A 650b city tourer with fenders and a front rack, a light touring bike, a basket city bike and a porteur.

Road and allroad

A 700c randonneur, a 650b allroad frameset and a big tire road bike.

Single speed and fixed

A 650b single speed with 48mm tires and a fixed gear bike.
