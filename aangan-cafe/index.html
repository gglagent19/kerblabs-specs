The idea

A first impression that fits the room.

Aangan Cafe is an independent cafe in Civil Lines, Jalandhar, known on Instagram for live music and workshops (about 6.5K followers). Today its only home online is the Instagram profile. This concept shows how the same cafe could greet a new guest on the web: warm, easy to find, and one tap from saying hello.

Live music, front and centre

A place to show what is on and when, once the cafe shares its schedule.
