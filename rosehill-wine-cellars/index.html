Rosehill Wine Cellars

Their site says they have been designing and building custom wine cellars since 1995, with a Canadian showroom in Mississauga and a USA office and showroom at 3940 Prospect Ave, #103, Naples, FL. Custom cellars follow personalized consultations, CAD designs and in-house construction managed from concept to completion.
