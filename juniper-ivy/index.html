The Studio

Slow boudoir, by design.

Juniper and Ivy Boudoir is a 5-star, women-owned, veteran-owned studio in Elkridge, Maryland — just outside Baltimore. Sessions are booked, not browsed; the work is editorial; the room is built so the woman in front of the camera leaves more like herself than less.

The Duo

Two women, one room.

Reviewers name them by their first names — Joni for the camera, Virginia for the hair and makeup. The whole experience runs through these two hands.

Joni

Photographer · Founder

Joni leads the session — consultation by phone, posing guidance in the room, the steady hand that, in one reviewer’s words, “immediately put me at ease.” Posing direction is mentioned by name in 5 separate reviews.

Virginia

Hair & Makeup Artistry
