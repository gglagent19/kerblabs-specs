Agent-Ready included

Future-proofing for the buyer who asks an AI assistant first.

AI assistants read websites differently from people. This build includes the files that let them describe Beverly Hills Car Club correctly and point a buyer to a quote or an offer.

  • llms.txt: services and sell / ask / rental / shipping actions in plain language
  • robots.txt: AI crawlers and AI search bots explicitly allowed (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and more)
  • JSON-LD schema: AutoDealer with name and location
  • Buy / sell / rent / ship services each listed as their own action
  • Contact actions written as clear, crawlable text
  • At launch: schema checked in Google Rich Results Test and files confirmed reachable on the live domain

Included in the concept as readiness for how people increasingly search. No traffic or ranking promises. The llms.txt, robots.txt and JSON-LD sit alongside this page in the build folder.
