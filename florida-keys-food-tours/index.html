Eat your way through the Keys

Florida Keys Food Tours runs in Islamorada and Key Largo. Their site lists nine bookable experiences, from a 1.5 hour fish cooking class to a golf cart food tour. Most book online through FareHarbor; the Key Largo Food Tour books by phone.

On land

The Original Islamorada Food Tour, from $119, 4 hours, up to 12 people. Golf cart tours from $179.

On the water

The Islamorada Sunset Boat Cruise Food Tour, 3 hours, up to 6 people, listed from $1,400.

In the kitchen

The Florida Keys Fish Cooking Class, from $99, 1.5 hours, up to 20 people.
