FAQ

Quick answers

Where does the boat leave from?

The dock at the end of Evernia Street on Flagler Drive, West Palm Beach. Peanut Island charters leave from Sailfish Marina.

What boat is it?

Carpe Vita, a 42-ft Fountaine Pajot Venezia catamaran, captained by Cal and Marty Landau.

How many guests?

Up to 6 guests. Overnight charters are limited to 2 guests.

What does it cost?

The current site lists Day Cruise at $350 per hour, Sunday Brunch $1,075 to $1,500, Sunset Dinner $1,250 to $1,700 and the 3-day Keys trip at $5,000. Confirm final pricing with the captains.
